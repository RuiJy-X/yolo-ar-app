# Editable packaging in Canva

[Open the editable Canva design](https://www.canva.com/d/2HwbwKC_f4AsCBM)

Pages, in order:
1. Front
2. Back
3. Left side
4. Right side

Each panel occupies its own A4 editing sheet. Proposed panel dimensions are 160 × 240 mm for the front and back and 30 × 240 mm for the sides. These are provisional, not printer specifications.

Verified in Canva: all four pages are editable; packaging copy exists as native text fields; original drone logo, original wordmark, actual application screenshot, and contour artwork are separate replaceable image elements. The images' internal pixels and contours are not individual text or vector elements.
